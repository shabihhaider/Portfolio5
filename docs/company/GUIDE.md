# How to Use These Documents — Quick Guide

This is the only file you need to open first. Find your situation below, go to the file it points to, read only what's relevant.

---

## By Situation

### "I just got a new client inquiry"
1. Read their message carefully — what do they need?
2. Send them the **Client Questionnaire** → [`onboarding-checklist.md`](./onboarding-checklist.md) (scroll to "Client Questionnaire Template")
3. Once they reply, scope the project → [`scope-of-work.md`](./scope-of-work.md)
4. Quote them correctly → [`pricing-policy.md`](./pricing-policy.md)

---

### "I need to send a quote / proposal"
- **What to charge:** [`pricing-policy.md`](./pricing-policy.md)
- **What to include in the scope:** [`scope-of-work.md`](./scope-of-work.md)
- **If it's an Upwork proposal:** [`upwork-rules.md`](./upwork-rules.md) — check the fee math before quoting

---

### "Client accepted — ready to start"
Go through **Stage 1 and Stage 2** of [`onboarding-checklist.md`](./onboarding-checklist.md) in order:
- Send Client Agreement → [`client-agreement.md`](./client-agreement.md)
- Send Welcome Email → [`onboarding-checklist.md`](./onboarding-checklist.md) (scroll to "Welcome Email Template")
- Collect deposit — **do not start without it**

---

### "Client is asking for something extra / not in the original scope"
→ [`revision-change-policy.md`](./revision-change-policy.md)

Read the **"Revision vs. Change Request"** table at the top.
- If it's a revision: track which round (1 or 2)
- If it's a change: use the **Change Request Form Template** in that file

---

### "Client used both revision rounds and wants more changes"
→ [`revision-change-policy.md`](./revision-change-policy.md)

Scroll to **"What Happens After 2 Rounds"** — charge $50/hr, minimum 1 hour. Tell them before starting.

---

### "I'm about to deliver the project"
→ [`delivery-handoff.md`](./delivery-handoff.md)

Go through the **Pre-Delivery QA checklist** top to bottom. Only send the delivery message after every item is checked. Use the **Final Delivery Message Template** in that file.

---

### "Client wants to cancel the project"
→ [`refund-cancellation-policy.md`](./refund-cancellation-policy.md)

Find the section that matches the situation:
- Cancelled before work started → full refund
- Cancelled after work started → deposit kept
- Cancelled after delivery → no refund

---

### "Client is unhappy / threatening a refund"
1. Stay calm — read [`communication-standards.md`](./communication-standards.md) → "Handling Unhappy Clients"
2. Check if their complaint is valid → [`revision-change-policy.md`](./revision-change-policy.md) (is it a bug or a preference change?)
3. If it's a real bug → fix it free within 30 days (see [`delivery-handoff.md`](./delivery-handoff.md) → "30-Day Warranty Period")
4. If they want a refund → [`refund-cancellation-policy.md`](./refund-cancellation-policy.md)

---

### "Client has gone silent / not responding"
→ [`communication-standards.md`](./communication-standards.md)

Scroll to **"Handling Non-Responsive Clients"** — follow the Day 3 / Day 7 / Day 14 / Day 30 script exactly.

---

### "Client hasn't paid the final invoice"
→ [`communication-standards.md`](./communication-standards.md)

Scroll to **"Handling Payment Issues"** — follow the Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 14 / Day 21 script. Do not hand over credentials until paid.

---

### "Client is asking who owns the code"
→ [`ip-confidentiality.md`](./ip-confidentiality.md)

Short answer: they own it after full payment. Until then, you do. Read the **"Rule: Payment = Transfer of Ownership"** section for the full explanation.

---

### "Client wants to keep the project confidential / not in my portfolio"
→ [`ip-confidentiality.md`](./ip-confidentiality.md)

Scroll to **"Portfolio Rights"** — they must request confidentiality in writing before the SOW is signed.

---

### "Client wants to sign an NDA before sharing details"
→ [`ip-confidentiality.md`](./ip-confidentiality.md)

Scroll to **"NDA Requests"** — you can sign a standard NDA. It works alongside the Client Agreement.

---

### "Client wants ongoing support after delivery"
→ [`maintenance-retainer.md`](./maintenance-retainer.md)

Pick the right tier based on what they need:
- WordPress updates / small fixes → **Tier 1 ($100/month)**
- Regular development work → **Tier 2 ($250/month)**
- Active product development → **Tier 3 ($500/month)**

Use the **pitch script** at the bottom of that file.

---

### "I'm sending a proposal on Upwork"
→ [`upwork-rules.md`](./upwork-rules.md)

Read **"Proposals"** section for structure. Check **"Pricing on Upwork"** for the fee math (add 15% to your target amount). Check **"Identifying Problem Clients"** before accepting any job.

---

### "I'm not sure how much to charge for this project"
→ [`pricing-policy.md`](./pricing-policy.md)

Start with the **Base Prices** table, then go through **"Price Multipliers"** for anything that adds complexity. Check **"Upwork vs. Direct Pricing"** if it's an Upwork job.

---

### "Something happened that's not covered anywhere"
→ [`client-agreement.md`](./client-agreement.md)

This is the master agreement. Most edge cases are covered in the sections on cancellation, liability, scope, and governing law.

## The 3 Rules That Matter Most

If you remember nothing else from these documents, remember these:

1. **No deposit = no work starts.** Ever. No exceptions.
2. **Everything in writing.** Verbal agreements do not exist.
3. **Scope changes = new quote.** "Just a quick addition" is how projects become unprofitable.
