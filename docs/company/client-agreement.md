# Client Agreement — Shabih.

> **How to use this document:** Before starting any project, share this agreement with the client and get written confirmation (email or Upwork message) that they have read and accepted these terms. For Upwork clients, the Upwork contract serves as the binding agreement — reference this document as the working terms.

---

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between **Muhammad Shabih Haider** (trading as Shabih., "Developer") and the client ("Client"), as identified at the time of project commencement.

---

## 2. Scope of Work

The project scope is defined in the Scope of Work (SOW) document or the project brief agreed upon before work begins. Work outside the agreed scope is not included and requires a separate Change Order (see Section 8).

The Developer will not begin work until:
- The SOW or project brief is agreed upon in writing
- The 50% deposit has been received and confirmed

---

## 3. Payment Terms

- **Deposit:** 50% of the total project fee is due before work begins
- **Final payment:** 50% is due upon delivery of the completed project
- **Deposit is non-refundable** once work has started (covers discovery, planning, and initial build)
- **Work pauses** if the final payment is not received within 7 days of delivery
- **Currency:** All prices are in USD unless otherwise agreed in writing
- **Accepted payment methods:** Upwork milestones, Payoneer, bank transfer (SWIFT)

For Upwork projects, all payments must go through Upwork's milestone system. Payments must not be moved off-platform during the first engagement with any client.

---

## 4. Timeline

The estimated timeline is agreed upon in the project brief. Timelines are based on:
- Receiving all required assets (logo, copy, images, credentials) from the client within 2 business days of kickoff
- Receiving consolidated revision feedback within 3 business days per round

**Timeline delays caused by late client feedback are not the Developer's responsibility.** If client feedback is delayed by more than 7 days, the project may be rescheduled and a re-activation fee may apply.

---

## 5. Revisions

- **2 rounds of revisions** are included in every project
- A revision round = one consolidated list of feedback submitted in a single message or document
- Rolling, piecemeal comments (e.g., "also change this... and now this...") do not count as a single round until the client confirms the round is complete
- Revisions must be within the original scope — changes that add new features or pages are Change Requests (see Section 8)
- Additional revision rounds beyond 2 are charged at the rate agreed in the project brief

---

## 6. Client Responsibilities

The Client is responsible for:
- Providing all content, images, brand assets, and credentials on time
- Designating one point of contact who has final approval authority
- Responding to requests for feedback within 3 business days
- Testing and approving the final delivery within 5 business days

Delays caused by the Client's failure to meet these responsibilities do not entitle the Client to a refund or an extended deadline at no extra cost.

---

## 7. Delivery & Acceptance

Upon delivery, the Client has **5 business days** to review and either:
- Approve the delivery (project is complete)
- Submit revision feedback (initiates Revision Round 1 or 2)

If no response is received within 5 business days of delivery, the project is considered **accepted and complete**. The final payment becomes due immediately.

---

## 8. Change Requests

Any request that adds features, pages, integrations, or functionality beyond the agreed SOW is a **Change Request**, not a revision.

Change Requests require:
1. Written description of the additional work
2. A new quote from the Developer
3. Written approval from the Client
4. Additional payment (agreed amount) before the new work begins

Change Requests may also affect the project timeline.

---

## 9. Intellectual Property

- All code, design, and assets created for the project transfer to the Client upon receipt of **full payment**
- Until full payment is received, the Developer retains all rights to the work
- The Developer retains the right to display the completed project in their portfolio unless the Client requests confidentiality in writing before project start
- The Developer retains ownership of any proprietary tools, libraries, or frameworks they developed independently that were incorporated into the project. The Client receives a perpetual, royalty-free license to use the final product — not the underlying tools

---

## 10. Confidentiality

The Developer agrees not to share, disclose, or publish any confidential client information (business data, unreleased products, private processes, credentials) to third parties during or after the project. This obligation survives for 2 years after project completion.

Confidentiality does not apply to:
- Information that is publicly available
- Information the Developer already knew prior to the engagement
- Disclosure required by law

---

## 11. Warranties & Liability

- The Developer warrants that the delivered work will function as described in the SOW at the time of delivery
- The Developer does not warrant compatibility with future third-party updates (e.g., WordPress core updates, plugin changes, API changes) after delivery
- The Developer's total liability is capped at the amount paid for the project
- The Developer is not liable for indirect losses, lost profits, or business interruption

---

## 12. Cancellation

- **Client cancels before work starts:** Full deposit refunded
- **Client cancels after work starts:** Deposit is forfeited. If more than 50% of work is complete, a partial payment may be required based on work delivered
- **Developer cancels for any reason:** Full deposit refunded within 7 business days

---

## 13. Abandoned Projects

If the Client does not respond to messages for **30 consecutive days**, the project is considered abandoned. The deposit is retained as compensation for time invested. The project may be re-activated for a re-activation fee.

---

## 14. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan, under the jurisdiction of Lahore, Punjab. Disputes will first be resolved through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved, the parties agree to arbitration before pursuing legal action.

---

## 15. Entire Agreement

This document, together with the agreed Scope of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. It supersedes any prior verbal or informal discussions.
