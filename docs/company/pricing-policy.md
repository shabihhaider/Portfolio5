# Pricing Policy — Shabih.

> **Internal reference.** Use this document when quoting projects to stay consistent and avoid undercharging. Do not share this document with clients — share the relevant pricing from the website instead.

---

## Base Prices (Floor — Never Go Below These)

| Service | Floor Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Landing Page | $400 | Single-page, React/Next.js, up to 6 sections |
| WordPress | $50 | Smallest fix or theme tweak only |
| AI Integration | $800 | Single API integration (e.g., OpenAI chatbot) |
| Custom Development | $1,500 | Simple full-stack app, no complex auth or payments |

These are **starting prices**, not flat rates. Always scope the project before quoting.

## Price Multipliers (What Increases the Price)

### Landing Pages
- Each additional page beyond 1: +$100–$200
- Custom animations (Framer Motion, GSAP): +$100–$200
- Blog/CMS integration: +$300
- Payment integration (Stripe): +$200
- Copywriting (if client can't provide content): not offered — refer out
- Rush delivery (under 7 days): +25–50%

### WordPress
- Theme customization (minor): $50–$150
- Theme customization (major rebuild): $200–$500
- Plugin development (custom): $300–$800
- WooCommerce setup (basic): $200–$400
- WooCommerce setup (advanced, payment gateways, subscriptions): $500–$1,000
- Site migration: $100–$200

### AI Integrations
- Single chatbot with basic responses (OpenAI): $800–$1,200
- RAG system (custom knowledge base): $1,200–$2,000
- Multi-model setup or agent workflows: $1,500–$3,000
- Dashboard or admin panel to manage AI: +$400–$800

### Custom Development
- Landing page + backend (contact form, auth): $1,500–$2,500
- Full SaaS MVP (auth, database, payments, dashboard): $3,000–$6,000+
- Complex scope: requires discovery call and detailed SOW before quoting

## Rush Fee Policy

If a client needs delivery faster than the standard timeline:
- 1–3 days faster: +25% surcharge
- Same-day or next-day: not offered (not realistic with day job)

Always be honest about capacity before accepting rush work.

## Upwork vs. Direct Pricing

**Upwork pricing should be ~15% higher** than direct pricing to account for:
- Upwork variable service fee: 0–15% per contract (shown at proposal time, averages 12–13%)
- Platform overhead and bidding time

**Rule:** Add 15% to your target amount to arrive at your Upwork quote.

Example:
- Direct price for landing page: $400
- Upwork quote: $460 (you receive ~$400 after ~13% fee)

Check the actual fee shown on each proposal page — if Upwork shows a lower rate, adjust your quote down accordingly.

---

## Discounts

Never offer discounts proactively. If a client asks:
- First-time client discount: maximum 10%, only if they are willing to provide a testimonial/review
- Referral discount: 10% on their next project if their referral becomes a paying client
- Long-term retainer: monthly rate negotiated separately (see maintenance retainer doc)

Never discount below the floor prices. Discounting signals low confidence and attracts problem clients.

---

## Payment Structure

- **Always:** 50% upfront deposit before any work begins
- **Always:** 50% final payment on delivery, before handing over code/credentials
- **No exceptions.** Work does not start without the deposit, no matter how trustworthy the client seems

For milestone-based projects (custom dev, large AI integrations):
- 30% on project start
- 30% on mid-project milestone (e.g., design approval or first functional build)
- 40% on final delivery

---

## Hourly Rate (Ad-Hoc Work)

For work outside the scope of a fixed-price project, or for clients who prefer hourly billing:
- **Hourly rate: $50/hr** (minimum 1 hour billing)

This should only be used for:
- Change requests on fixed-price projects
- Maintenance tasks beyond the free support period
- Consulting calls beyond 30 minutes

---

## What to Do When a Client's Budget Is Too Low

If a client's budget is below your floor price:
1. Do not lower your price to match their budget
2. Explain what can be done within their budget (reduced scope)
3. If no reduced scope is viable, politely decline

**Script:** *"My starting price for [service] is $X. That covers [what's included]. If your budget is lower, I could offer [reduced scope version] for $Y. If that doesn't work for you, I understand — feel free to reach out when you're ready to move forward."*
