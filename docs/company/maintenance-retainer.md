# Maintenance & Retainer Policy — Shabih.

---

## Post-Delivery Support (Free — 30 Days)

Every project includes **30 days of free bug fixes** after final delivery and acceptance. This covers:
- Bugs in the delivered code that prevent it from working as agreed in the SOW
- Broken functionality that worked at the time of delivery but stopped working due to issues in the code

This does **not** cover:
- New feature requests
- Third-party service outages or breaking changes (e.g., a plugin update breaks the site)
- Issues caused by client modifications to the code after handoff
- Content updates or copy changes

After 30 days, all support is billed at the standard rate or covered by a retainer.

---

## Hourly Support (Ad-Hoc)

For clients who need occasional help after the 30-day free period:
- **Rate:** $50/hr
- **Minimum:** 1 hour per request
- **Turnaround:** 1–3 business days for minor tasks, longer for larger work
- **How to request:** Email or Upwork message describing the task. Developer will estimate hours before beginning.

Best for: small fixes, content updates, plugin updates, minor feature additions.

---

## Monthly Maintenance Retainer

For clients who want ongoing support without worrying about hourly billing.

### Tier 1 — Basic ($100/month)
Best for: WordPress sites, simple landing pages that need regular upkeep

Includes:
- WordPress core, theme, and plugin updates
- Monthly site backup
- Uptime monitoring (notify client of downtime)
- Up to 1 hour of minor content or copy changes
- 48-hour response time for support requests

### Tier 2 — Standard ($250/month)
Best for: Active sites with regular update needs or ongoing feature development

Includes everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Up to 3 hours of development work (bug fixes, small features, adjustments)
- Priority response time (24 hours)
- Monthly performance check (Lighthouse, Core Web Vitals)
- 1 minor SEO update per month

### Tier 3 — Growth ($500/month)
Best for: Clients actively building or scaling their product

Includes everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Up to 8 hours of development work
- Same-day response time during working hours (PKT)
- Monthly strategy call (30 min) to review priorities
- Basic analytics review and recommendations

---

## Retainer Terms

- Billed monthly, in advance
- Payment via Upwork recurring contract, Payoneer, or bank transfer
- Unused hours do not roll over to the next month
- Either party may cancel with 14 days written notice
- Retainer work is scheduled in advance — it does not guarantee same-day emergency fixes (see Tier 3 for priority access)

---

## When to Offer a Retainer

Offer a retainer at project close if:
- The client has a WordPress site that will need regular updates
- The client is building a product and will need ongoing development
- The client has expressed interest in future features
- The client seems like a long-term partner (good communication, fair expectations)

**Script for offering a retainer:**

*"Now that [Project Name] is live, you might find it useful to have someone on standby for updates and improvements. I offer monthly maintenance packages starting at $100/month that cover [brief description]. Would that be useful for you?"*

Do not push it. Offer once, note the response, and follow up after 30 days if they haven't responded.
