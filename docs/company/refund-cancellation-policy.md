# Refund & Cancellation Policy — Shabih.

---

## Core Principle

The 50% deposit exists to compensate for time spent on discovery, planning, and early build work — none of which can be refunded once spent. The final 50% is earned on delivery of the agreed work.

---

## Cancellation by Client

### Before Work Starts
If the client cancels before the Developer begins any work:
- **Full deposit refunded** within 7 business days
- "Work started" means the Developer has begun discovery, planning, wireframing, or coding

### After Work Starts (Less than 50% complete)
- **Deposit is non-refundable**
- No charge for the remaining balance
- Any work completed up to that point is forfeited by the client — no partial deliverables are handed over

### After Work Starts (More than 50% complete)
- **Deposit is non-refundable**
- A partial payment for work completed may be invoiced, calculated as:
  *% of project completed × remaining 50%*
- Partial deliverables may be provided at the Developer's discretion

### After Final Delivery
- **No refunds after the client approves or accepts the final delivery**
- If the client accepts delivery (explicitly or by not responding within 5 business days), the project is considered complete
- Final payment is due immediately upon acceptance

---

## Cancellation by Developer

If the Developer must cancel a project for any reason:
- **Full refund of any amounts paid** within 7 business days
- Any work completed will be handed over to the client at no charge

The Developer will only cancel in exceptional circumstances (medical emergency, force majeure, etc.) and will communicate as early as possible.

---

## Refund for Defective Delivery

If the delivered work does not meet the specifications in the agreed SOW:
1. The client must raise the issue in writing within **5 business days of delivery**
2. The Developer will fix the defect at no charge (this is warranty work, not a revision)
3. If the defect cannot be resolved after reasonable attempts, a partial refund proportional to the undelivered scope may be issued

A "defect" is a specific failure to meet the SOW. Design preferences or features not in the SOW are not defects.

---

## No-Refund Situations

No refund will be issued for:
- Change of mind after work has started
- Client decided to go with a different developer
- Client's business circumstances changed
- Client does not like the result but it matches the agreed SOW
- Delays caused by the client's failure to provide assets or feedback on time
- Third-party service outages or price changes (Vercel, OpenAI, Stripe, etc.)

---

## Abandoned Projects

A project is considered **abandoned** if the client does not respond to communications for **30 consecutive days**.

On abandonment:
- The deposit is retained in full
- No further work is performed
- The project is closed

**Re-activation:** If the client returns after abandonment and wants to restart the project, a **re-activation fee of $100** applies before work resumes. This covers time spent re-reviewing the project state.

---

## Dispute Resolution

If a client disputes a payment or charges back through Upwork, PayPal, or their bank:
1. First step: contact the Developer directly at shabihhaider191@gmail.com to resolve the issue
2. The Developer will provide documentation of all work completed and communications
3. If unresolved after 7 days of good-faith negotiation, the parties may escalate to platform arbitration (Upwork) or legal arbitration (Pakistan, Lahore jurisdiction)

Chargebacks initiated without prior communication will be contested with full documentation.
