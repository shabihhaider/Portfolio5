# Client Onboarding Checklist — Shabih.

> Use this checklist for every new project. Go through it in order. Do not skip steps.

---

## Stage 1: Pre-Project (Before Deposit)

- [ ] Receive and review client's project inquiry
- [ ] Clarify scope: what exactly do they need? What's the goal?
- [ ] Confirm the service type and rough budget alignment
- [ ] Send the **Client Questionnaire** (see template below)
- [ ] Review questionnaire responses — identify any red flags (unrealistic budget, vague scope, unclear decision-maker)
- [ ] Prepare and send the **Scope of Work** document
- [ ] Send the **Client Agreement** or reference it in the SOW
- [ ] Confirm the client has read and accepted the terms (written confirmation in email or Upwork message)
- [ ] Send the **deposit invoice** (50% of total)

**Do not begin any work until the deposit is received and confirmed.**

---

## Stage 2: Kickoff (After Deposit Clears)

- [ ] Confirm deposit received (check Upwork, Payoneer, or bank)
- [ ] Send **Welcome Email** (see template below)
- [ ] Request all required assets (list from SOW)
- [ ] Set a deadline for asset delivery (2 business days from kickoff)
- [ ] Create a shared folder (Google Drive or Notion) for the project
- [ ] Add project to your tracker: client name, service, total value, deposit date, deadline
- [ ] Begin work only after assets are received

---

## Stage 3: Active Project

- [ ] Send a Day 1 update: *"I've started work on [X]. Here's what I'm doing today."*
- [ ] Send daily or every-other-day progress updates (especially for projects over 1 week)
- [ ] Flag any blockers immediately — don't wait if you're stuck on a client asset
- [ ] Track which revision round you're on
- [ ] Remind the client before their final revision round: *"This is your last included revision round."*

---

## Stage 4: Delivery

- [ ] Complete the **Delivery & Handoff Checklist** (see delivery-handoff.md)
- [ ] Send the **final delivery message** with:
  - Link to the live project or staging environment
  - Summary of what was built
  - Instructions for using/managing it (brief, or link to documentation)
  - Reminder that the client has 5 business days to review
  - Final invoice (50% remaining)
- [ ] Confirm final payment received before handing over credentials (logins, API keys, repo access)

---

## Stage 5: Post-Project

- [ ] Transfer ownership: repo, domain, hosting, API keys
- [ ] Send **testimonial request** (see template below)
- [ ] Offer maintenance retainer (see maintenance-retainer.md)
- [ ] Archive the project in your tracker with final payment date and notes
- [ ] Add to portfolio (unless client requested confidentiality)

---

## Client Questionnaire Template

Send this before writing any SOW. Copy and paste into email or Upwork message.

---

*Hi [Name],*

*Thanks for reaching out! Before I put together a scope and quote, I have a few quick questions:*

1. *What is the name/website of your business?*
2. *What are you looking to build? (Brief description)*
3. *What's the main goal of the project? (More sales, more leads, automate a process, etc.)*
4. *Do you have existing branding? (Logo, colors, fonts — yes/no)*
5. *Do you have content ready? (All text, images — yes/no, or partially)*
6. *Are there any websites or designs you like as reference?*
7. *Do you have a deadline or launch date in mind?*
8. *What is your budget for this project?*
9. *Who will be the main point of contact? (And do they have final approval?)*
10. *How do you prefer to communicate? (Email, WhatsApp, Slack)*

*Looking forward to working with you!*

*Shabih*
*shabih.tech*
