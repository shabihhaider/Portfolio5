## Core Values

1. **Honesty** — Real pricing, real timelines, real capabilities. No overpromising.
2. **Direct communication** — Clients talk to the developer, not a middleman.
3. **Quality over speed** — Fast delivery is a goal, not an excuse for cutting corners.
4. **Transparency** — Fixed-price quotes before work starts. No surprises.
5. **Accountability** — Commitments are kept. If something changes, the client is told immediately.

## Operating Model

- **Capacity:** 4–5 hours per day available for client work
- **Response time:** Within 24 hours on any active project day
- **Payment:** 50% deposit upfront, 50% on delivery
- **Revisions:** 2 rounds included per project
- **Platforms:** Direct (shabih.tech) and Upwork
- **Currency:** USD for all projects

---

## What We Don't Do

- We do not take on more work than we can deliver on time
- We do not subcontract without client knowledge
- We do not work for clients who request changes after a signed scope without a formal change order
- We do not start work before receiving the deposit
